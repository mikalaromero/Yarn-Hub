About the Event

Yarn Jam is a hackathon-inspired crochet event that brings together creativity, community, and innovation. Participants are challenged to design and create unique crochet projects within a limited time, pushing their skills and imagination. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced fiber artist, Yarn Jam offers a space to experiment, collaborate, and turn simple yarn into something extraordinary.

What to Expect

  • Great Vibes Enjoy a welcoming and cozy atmosphere where you can relax, get creative, and feel inspired alongside others.
  • Crochet Workshops Participate in hands-on workshops where you’ll learn new techniques, start fun projects, and improve your skills—no experience needed!
  • Cool Prizes Take part in exciting challenges and activities for a chance to win fun and creative prizes throughout the event.

Event Schedule

Day 1 - Kickoff: Get Hooked!!

Kick things off with introductions, team formation, and the official theme reveal. Meet fellow makers, get inspired, and start your first stitches.


Day 2 - The Stitch Sprint

Dive into the main build phase. Bring your ideas to life through focused creation, collaboration, and a full day of stitching, experimenting, and pushing your skills.


Day 3 - Final Loop: The Showcase

Wrap up your project and share your work. Participants present their creations, celebrate each other’s progress, and compete for awards in a final showcase.


RSVP
